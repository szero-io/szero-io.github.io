Manifesto

S0 is the initial state — the starting point of every system before its first action, its first observation, its first reward signal. Every capable machine was once nothing. We build the layer that changes that.

Good decisions become part of the system. A system needs state, action, feedback, constraints, and a way to improve while human judgment stays in the loop.

Our direction is simple: help humans and machines make better decisions, expand human agency, and turn experience into knowledge the next decision can use.

Core Capabilities

Systems for better decisions

01

Decision Policies

Learned policies for recurring choices. The system reads the state, weighs constraints, acts, and improves from the result.

02

System Coordination

Many parts working toward one objective. We coordinate tasks, resources, and tradeoffs across fleets, pipelines, and human-machine operations.

03

Transfer & Adaptation

Moving policies from controlled tests to live operations. Simulation, offline data, and human feedback reduce the gap between training and deployment.

04

Edge Deployment

Inference close to the decision. On-device for robots and on-prem for operations that need low latency, clear control, and stable service.

05

Data & Perception

Turning raw signals into useful state. Sensors, cameras, documents, and event streams become the context a system needs to choose well.

06

Continuous Improvement

Systems that learn from use. Feedback from people, machines, and outcomes keeps policies useful within clear safety bounds.

How We Work

Decision-grade engineering

01

Define the MDP

Every deployment starts by defining the decision: state, action, reward, constraint, owner, and feedback.

02

We test policies in simulation, historical data, and controlled pilots before they affect live operations.

03

Transfer & validate

We move from test to production through a clear curriculum and validate against real edge cases.

04

Deploy & improve

Feedback keeps policies useful in production. The system improves while teams keep control of the bounds.
