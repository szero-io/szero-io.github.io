Why Szero

The problem selects the technology.

We start with the decision, environment, and required outcome, not a preferred model.

Foundation models and agents can be powerful components. They are not the company identity or the right answer to every problem. Szero combines the methods and software the work actually requires.

  1. 01Optimization and controlFor constrained decisions, planning, allocation, and operational performance.
  2. 02Reinforcement learningWhen actions create measurable feedback and policies can improve through experience.
  3. 03Simulation and specialized modelsFor testing scenarios, representing domain structure, and evaluating choices before they matter.
  4. 04Foundation models and conventional softwareFor language, reasoning, tools, workflows, interfaces, integrations, and dependable execution.

How work starts

From a hard problem to a working system.

Frame the opportunity

Define the decision or workflow, who it serves, the constraints, available evidence, and the outcome worth improving.

Design the system

Select the right methods, architecture, integrations, evaluation plan, and level of human involvement.

Build and prove

Deliver a focused pilot, test it against realistic conditions, and use results to decide what should become a production system or product.

Start with consulting, a pilot, or a defined product opportunity. The first step should reduce uncertainty and produce something useful.
